How it works

  1. Enter the web page you want to open after each scan in URL template.
  2. Where the barcode number should go, type {scannedBarcode} . https://example.com/search?barcode={scannedBarcode}
  3. Tap Templates to pick a saved URL pattern, or Save configuration, then scan.
What if I don’t use {scannedBarcode}?

The tool adds ?code= and the scanned value to the end of your URL automatically (or &code= if your URL already has a ?).

Advanced: share a link with the URL already filled in

After entering your URL template, tap the link icon beside the field to copy a template share link. Anyone who opens that link gets your template pre-filled on this scanner page.
